## 1. Release Overview

We are pleased to introduce **UCPSF v1.0.0**, the initial research release of:

**Universal Control Planes and Scoring Tree/Forest/Folding for Structural Intelligence.**

UCPSF proposes a unified architectural framework for understanding, organizing, and improving the evaluation and control mechanisms of increasingly complex AI systems.

Modern AI workflows—including multi-agent systems, AI Relay architectures, hybrid intelligence systems, and recursive self-improvement processes—frequently depend on many local, intermediate, and global evaluation mechanisms.

These mechanisms determine:

- Which computational paths should be explored;
- Which results should be accepted or rejected;
- Which intelligence engines should be selected;
- Which structural modifications should be retained;
- Which improvements should be promoted;
- Which actions require authorization or escalation.

UCPSF investigates the possibility that these evaluation mechanisms are not merely auxiliary scoring functions.

Instead, they may form a reusable, composable, learnable, and evolving class of intelligence structures.

The central proposition is:

> **Scoring Intelligence can become an explicit structural intelligence capability—one that evaluates, composes, selects, and improves the mechanisms governing intelligent computation.**

The framework further proposes a **Universal Control Plane** for coordinating these mechanisms with heterogeneous intelligence engines, evidence, policy, and governed structural evolution.

## 2. Two Major Research Pillars

### Pillar I — Generalized Scoring Intelligence

UCPSF extends evaluation beyond isolated scoring functions.

It introduces a conceptual progression:

```text id="w5ew0q"
Primitive Scoring
       |
       v
Generalized Scoring Intelligence
       |
       v
Scoring Trees and Forests
       |
       v
Structural Folding
       |
       v
Common Concept Core Extraction
       |
       v
Recursive CCC Distillation
       |
       v
Perspective-Aware Meta-Scoring
```

This research pillar investigates how scoring mechanisms may become:

- Structurally organized;
- Compositionally reusable;
- Perspective-sensitive;
- Evidence-driven;
- Adaptively selected;
- Recursively improvable.

Three proposed Scoring Operator families provide an initial design vocabulary:

1. **Value-Based Folding**
2. **Branching-Based Folding**
3. **ANN Scoring Nodes**

The framework also explores **Unaligned AND Folding**, a proposed mechanism for extracting commonality across heterogeneous evaluation structures without requiring direct node-to-node alignment.

A related research direction is the extraction of reusable **Scoring Common Concept Cores (CCCs)**.

These CCCs may support structural reuse through a **Core±Delta** model, where shared evaluation mechanisms coexist with specialized differences.

### Pillar II — Universal Control Plane and Dual-Hybrid Intelligence

UCPSF also investigates a second architectural direction.

Large AI models often integrate contextual localization and task computation within a tightly coupled architecture.

UCPSF proposes studying their functional decomposition into:

- **Localization Intelligence:** Identifying, retrieving, and organizing task-relevant context and structural pathways.
- **Per-Node Intelligence:** Performing reasoning, scoring, generation, verification, simulation, or other specialized computation.

Potential Localization mechanisms include Transformer-based contextual processing, retrieval, Metric Differential Trees (MDT), Universal Tree Nodes (UTN), Common Concept Cores, and Calling Graphs.

Potential Per-Node mechanisms include LLMs, specialized Transformers, ANN evaluators, Principle Engines, symbolic systems, and verification tools.

The proposed **Dual-Hybrid Intelligence** architecture investigates adaptive coupling and decoupling of these capabilities.

A Universal Control Plane would coordinate their selection, composition, evaluation, and governance.

## 3. Key Research Contributions

### 3.1 Generalized Scoring Intelligence

Proposes treating scoring as a structured intelligence capability rather than only a fixed numerical function.

### 3.2 Scoring Trees and Forests

Introduces hierarchical and composite representations for organizing evaluation mechanisms across complex workflows.

### 3.3 Three Scoring Operator Families

Identifies Value-Based Folding, Branching-Based Folding, and ANN Scoring Nodes as an initial operator taxonomy.

### 3.4 Unaligned AND Folding

Proposes extracting reusable evaluation commonality from heterogeneous structures with different organizations and representations.

### 3.5 Recursive CCC Distillation

Investigates how repeated evaluation structures may yield reusable Common Concept Cores with explicit applicability boundaries.

### 3.6 Perspective-Aware Meta-Scoring

Proposes selecting and evaluating evaluators according to goals, context, budget, time horizon, and risk.

### 3.7 Dual-Hybrid Intelligence

Explores adaptive composition of Localization Intelligence and Per-Node Intelligence.

### 3.8 Universal Control Plane

Proposes a coordinating architecture for execution, evaluation, evidence, resource management, governance, and structural learning.

### 3.9 Governed Structural RSI

Investigates recursive improvement of both Intelligence Architecture and Evaluation Architecture under independent validation and explicit authorization.

### 3.10 Open-Ended Structural Learning

Connects Scoring Intelligence with portable intelligence components, reusable CCCs, and collective structural evolution.

## 5. Scoring Intelligence and Structural RSI

One of the central research directions of UCPSF is the interaction between two evolutionary processes.

**Intelligence Architecture Evolution**

Improves the mechanisms that perform tasks.

**Evaluation Architecture Evolution**

Improves the mechanisms that evaluate tasks and structural modifications.

These processes may reinforce each other.

However, their interaction introduces risks of circular evaluation, metric exploitation, and self-referential validation.

UCPSF therefore emphasizes:

- Explicit scoring and composition contracts;
- Independent reference evaluation;
- Counter-evidence;
- Anti-Goodhart mechanisms;
- Controlled structural promotion;
- Authorization boundaries;
- Monitoring and rollback.

The proposed governance principle is:

> **Intelligence may generate and evaluate structural improvements, but structural promotion and external action must remain governed by explicit contracts, independent evidence, and authorized control.**

## 11. Research Scope and Limitations

**UCPSF v1.0.0 is a conceptual research and architecture release.**

It introduces hypotheses, architectural models, terminology, research questions, and proposed validation protocols.

It does **not** establish:

- A complete production runtime;
- A proven universal scoring algorithm;
- Empirical superiority of Scoring Forests;
- Guaranteed decision preservation under Folding;
- Universal transferability of CCCs;
- Guaranteed benefit from Hybrid Intelligence;
- Verified open-ended Structural RSI;
- Formal safety guarantees from the proposed Control Plane.

These are research questions for future investigation.

The word **Universal** describes the proposed extensibility and generality of the control architecture and contracts, not a claim that a single evaluation mechanism is optimal for every domain.
