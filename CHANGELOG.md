## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-15

### Initial Research Release

Established **UCPSF** as a unified, theory-first research framework connecting Generalized Scoring Intelligence, Scoring Tree/Forest/Folding, Hybrid Structural Intelligence, Universal Control Planes, and governed Structural Recursive Self-Improvement.

The release introduces the central research proposition:

> Scoring Intelligence itself can become a structurally learnable, transferable, and recursively improvable form of intelligence.

### Added — Foundational Research Concepts

- **Generalized Scoring Intelligence:** A proposed framework for treating evaluation as a structured and evolving intelligence capability.
- **Three Scoring Operator Families:** Value-Based Folding, Branching-Based Folding, and ANN Scoring Nodes.
- **Scoring Trees and Forests:** Structured representations of local, composite, and Perspective-specific evaluation mechanisms.
- **Unaligned AND Folding:** A proposed mechanism for extracting commonality across heterogeneous evaluation structures.
- **Recursive CCC Distillation:** Evidence-driven extraction and refinement of reusable Common Concept Cores.
- **Aspen Grove / Core±Delta Model:** Shared structural foundations with preserved specialized differences.
- **Perspective-Aware Meta-Scoring:** Selection and evaluation of evaluators according to goals, context, budget, time, and risk.
- **Dual-Hybrid Intelligence:** Adaptive composition of Localization and Per-Node Intelligence Engines.
- **Universal Control Plane:** A proposed logical architecture coordinating computation, evaluation, governance, and structural learning.
- **Governed Structural RSI:** Recursive structural modification under contracts, independent validation, authorization, monitoring, and rollback.

### Established — Research and Governance Principles

- Specifications and contracts before unrestricted runtime composition.
- Explicit separation of scoring and authorization.
- Independent evidence for evaluator and architecture improvement.
- Preservation of decision-relevant differences during Structural Folding.
- Perspective-aware optimization with mandatory constraints.
- Counter-evidence and anti-Goodhart evaluation.
- Governed promotion and rollback of structural modifications.
- Clear distinction between proposed mechanisms and validated results.

### Research Status

Version 1.0.0 is a **conceptual research and architecture release**.

It does not claim:

- Completed runtime implementation;
- Published benchmark results;
- Proven generality of the three Scoring Operator families;
- Guaranteed decision preservation under Folding;
- Universal transferability of CCCs;
- Superiority of Hybrid Intelligence over integrated models;
- Guaranteed beneficial Structural RSI;
- Formal safety guarantees from the Universal Control Plane.

These remain subjects for future formalization and independent validation.

## Versioning Policy

- **Major versions (X.0.0):** Significant changes to the conceptual framework, architectural assumptions, or research scope.
- **Minor versions (1.X.0):** New research papers, substantial conceptual extensions, or proposed mechanisms that remain compatible with the existing framework.
- **Patch versions (1.0.X):** Corrections, terminology clarifications, citation updates, and documentation fixes that do not materially change the research claims.

A version number identifies the state of the research publication; it does not imply implementation maturity or empirical validation.
