## 1. Purpose

This document defines the seven canonical architecture figures associated with the UCPSF research framework.

The figures illustrate:

1. The overall Universal Control Plane architecture;
2. Three proposed Scoring Operator families;
3. Scoring Trees, Forests, and shared structural roots;
4. Unaligned AND Folding and recursive CCC distillation;
5. Perspective-Aware Optimization and Meta-Scoring;
6. Dual-Hybrid Localization and Per-Node Intelligence;
7. Governed Structural Recursive Self-Improvement.

The figures are conceptual architecture illustrations.

They do not represent completed implementations, empirical results, or demonstrated universal performance guarantees.

## 4. Fig-002 — Three Universal Scoring Operators

**Filename:**

`figures/Fig-002-Three-Universal-Scoring-Operators.png`

### Concept

Fig-002 illustrates three proposed Scoring Operator families:

**Value-Based Folding**

Operations that adjust, combine, calibrate, normalize, or compress evaluative values and parameters.

**Branching-Based Folding**

Operations that reorganize, specialize, merge, or reduce branches in scoring and decision structures.

**ANN Scoring Nodes**

Learned scoring mechanisms supporting ranking, approximation, and specialized evaluation.

### Central Message

> Generalized Scoring Intelligence may be constructed from reusable value-level, branching-level, and learned scoring mechanisms.

The three families are candidate architectural abstractions rather than an exhaustive or mutually exclusive mathematical classification.

### Canonical Caption

**Fig-002. Three Universal Scoring Operator Families.** Proposed value-based, branching-based, and ANN-based mechanisms for constructing, adapting, and composing Scoring Intelligence structures.

## 8. Fig-006 — Dual-Hybrid Localization and Per-Node Intelligence

**Filename:**

`figures/Fig-006-Dual-Hybrid-Localization-and-Per-Node-Intelligence.png`

### Concept

Fig-006 illustrates two functional intelligence families.

**Localization Intelligence**

Candidate mechanisms include:

- Transformer-based contextual processing;
- Semantic retrieval;
- MDT;
- UTN-based localization;
- CCC matching;
- Calling Graph traversal.

**Per-Node Intelligence**

Candidate mechanisms include:

- General-purpose LLMs;
- Specialized Transformers;
- ANN Scoring Nodes;
- Principle Engines;
- Symbolic reasoning;
- Optimization and verification tools.

### Composition Model

\[
a=(L_i,I_j,C_{ij})
\]

where:

- \(L_i\): Localization Engine;
- \(I_j\): Per-Node Intelligence Engine;
- \(C_{ij}\): Composition contract.

### Central Message

> Localization Intelligence and Per-Node Intelligence can be treated as separately selectable functional capabilities, enabling adaptive composition of heterogeneous intelligence mechanisms.

This decomposition is functional. It does not imply that Transformer attention and computation are physically independent modules.

### Canonical Caption

**Fig-006. Dual-Hybrid Localization and Per-Node Intelligence.** Proposed architecture for selecting and composing heterogeneous Localization Engines and Per-Node Intelligence Engines through explicit contracts, Scoring Intelligence, and Universal Control Plane coordination.

## 12. Figure Naming and Repository Conventions

### 12.1 Canonical Naming

All figure filenames use:

`Fig-NNN-Descriptive-Title.png`

where:

- `Fig` identifies an architecture figure;
- `NNN` is a three-digit figure number;
- The title uses hyphen-separated words.

### 12.2 Canonical Directory

All figure assets belong in:

`figures/`

### 12.3 Stable References

Once the initial DOI release is archived, canonical figure identifiers should remain stable.

If a figure is revised substantially, the repository should record the change through release metadata or version history.

### 12.4 Figure Captions

Captions should:

- Identify the represented mechanism;
- Distinguish proposed architecture from validated results;
- Avoid unsupported performance claims;
- Remain consistent with the relevant research paper.

### 12.5 Markdown Paths

For root-level documents:

```markdown
![Figure Title](figures/Fig-NNN-Descriptive-Title.png)
```

For documents inside `docs/` or `cases/`:

