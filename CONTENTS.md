## 1. Repository Overview

This repository presents a research framework for **Generalized Scoring Intelligence, Scoring Tree/Forest/Folding, Hybrid Localization and Per-Node Intelligence, Universal Control Planes, and governed Structural Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI)**.

Its central proposition is:

> **Scoring Intelligence itself can become a structurally learnable, transferable, and recursively improvable form of intelligence.**

The research is organized around two complementary pillars:

1. **Generalized Scoring Intelligence** — How evaluation structures can be represented, composed, folded, reused, and improved.
2. **Hybrid Structural Intelligence** — How heterogeneous Localization and Per-Node Intelligence Engines can be selected, combined, evaluated, and governed.

The **Universal Control Plane (UCP)** connects these pillars through Perspective-aware selection, Meta-Scoring, explicit contracts, evidence management, governance, and structural evolution.

The initial release contains:

- 8 core research papers;
- 3 conceptual case studies;
- 7 architecture figures;
- Research navigation and supporting documentation;
- Release and citation metadata.

The initial release is theory-first. It does not claim completed implementation or empirical validation.

## 8. Key Concepts and Terminology

| Concept | Brief Description |
|---|---|
| **Scoring Intelligence** | Structured capability for evaluation, selection, and improvement of scoring mechanisms |
| **Scoring Tree** | Hierarchical or graph-structured evaluation mechanism |
| **Scoring Forest** | Collection of related or specialized Scoring Trees |
| **Value-Based Folding** | Adjustment or compression of scoring values and parameters |
| **Branching-Based Folding** | Reorganization or reduction of evaluation branches |
| **ANN Scoring Node** | Learned scoring mechanism |
| **Unaligned AND Folding** | Proposed extraction of commonality from structurally heterogeneous evaluators |
| **CCC** | Common Concept Core |
| **Core±Delta** | Shared reusable core with preserved specialized differences |
| **Perspective** | Goals, context, budget, time horizon, and risk preferences |
| **Meta-Scoring** | Evaluation of evaluators |
| **Localization Intelligence** | Selection and organization of relevant context |
| **Per-Node Intelligence** | Computation over localized context |
| **Dual-Hybrid Intelligence** | Adaptive composition of multiple Localization and Per-Node Engines |
| **Universal Control Plane** | Logical coordination of computation, scoring, governance, and structural evolution |
| **Structural RSI** | Recursive improvement of intelligence-system structures |
| **Counter-Evidence** | Evidence challenging a proposed evaluation or improvement |
| **Governed Promotion** | Authorized deployment of independently validated structural changes |

For extended definitions, see [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md).

## 12. Research Status and Validation Boundaries

The initial UCPSF release is a conceptual research framework.

Its documents include:

- Proposed architectural mechanisms;
- Formalization candidates;
- Conceptual algorithms;
- Research hypotheses;
- Case-study scenarios;
- Experimental validation designs.

The release does not establish:

- Universal superiority of Scoring Forests;
- General decision preservation under Structural Folding;
- Universal transferability of CCCs;
- Guaranteed effectiveness of Meta-Scoring;
- Superiority of modular Hybrid Intelligence;
- Guaranteed beneficial Structural RSI;
- Complete safety or governance guarantees.

These remain research questions requiring independent evidence.
